# ChatNexa - The Future of Conversational AI for Businesses Worldwide

ChatNexa is a next-generation AI platform empowering businesses globally to deliver instant, intelligent, and human-like customer support at scale.

🌍 **Global Problem**
Businesses worldwide lose over $75B annually due to slow customer response, high support costs, and lack of 24/7 availability. 73% of customers expect instant replies.

💡 **Our Solution**
ChatNexa is an omnichannel AI agent that integrates with WhatsApp, Instagram, Websites, Slack, and Messenger. Businesses can train it on their data (PDFs, URLs, Docs) in minutes - no code needed.

It speaks 100+ languages, understands context, and hands off to humans when needed.

🛠️ **Built on Microsoft Azure**
- **Azure OpenAI (GPT-4o)** - Advanced reasoning & conversation
- **Azure AI Search** - RAG for accurate knowledge retrieval
- **Azure Bot Service** - Global multi-channel deployment
- **Azure App Service, Cosmos DB & Functions** - Scalable, serverless backend
- **Azure Translator & Speech** - Real-time translation and voice AI
- **Azure AI Content Safety** - Responsible AI

✨ **Key Features**
- Omnichannel Support (WhatsApp, Web, IG, Messenger)
- 100+ Language Auto-Translation
- Custom Knowledge Base (RAG)
- Smart Lead Capture & CRM Integration
- Sentiment Analysis & Analytics
- Human Handoff
